SLIDE 1: Quark composition
-Hadrons are not fundamental particles, as they are a group of subatomic particles made up of smaller particles called quarks.
-Typed of Hadrons:
Baryons
Mesons

-Mesons: Made up of a quark and anti-quark pair (2 quarks)

SLIDE 2: Quark composition Baryons
-Baryons are made from 3 quarks Eg: proton, neutron, antiproton, antineutron

-Protons(UUD): are made up of two up quarks and a down quark
Total charge: 2/3e + 2/3e -1/3e = +e
-AntiProton: Made from (Anti-Up, Anti-Up, Anti-Down quarks)which the same quarks but with opposite charges
Total charge: -2/3e -2/3e +1/3e = -e

Neutrons(UDD): are made up of two down quarks and an up quark
Total charge: 2/3e -1/3e -1/3e = 0
-AntiProton: Made from (Anti-Up, Anti-Down, Anti-Down quarks)which the same quarks but with opposite charges
Total charge: -2/3e +1/3e +1/3e = 0

SLIDE 3:The Quark Model of Beta Decay(β- decay)
-Beta decay happens via the weak interaction
-β- decay is when a neutron turns into a proton emitting an electron and anti-electron neutrino
-specifically, a neutron turns into a proton because a down quark turns into an up quark

SLIDE 4:The Quark Model of Beta Decay(β+ decay)
-β+ decay is when a proton turns into a neutron emitting an positron and an electron neutrino
-specifically, a proton turns into a neutron because an up quark turns into a down quark

SLIDE 5:Fundamental particles-Leptons
-Leptons are also a group of fundamental particles
-This means they are not made up of any other particles (not made of any quarks)

There are six leptons altogether:
electron
electron neutrino
muon
muon neutrino
tau
tau neutrino

SLIDE 6:Leptons continuation
-The muon and tau particle are very similar to the electron but with slightly larger mass
-Electrons, muon and tau particles all have a charge of -1e 

-There are three flavours (types) of neutrinos (electron, muon, tau)
Neutrinos are the most abundant leptons in the universe
They have no charge and negligible mass (almost 0)

-Leptons interact with the weak interaction, electromagnetic and gravitational forces but not strong force. However quarks do.
